ABOUT SATYAJIT STUDY SPHERE

A space for better preparation.

This platform is being built as a practical, organised learning space for students preparing for competitive examinations. The goal is simple: make useful study material easier to find, understand, revise and apply.

As the site grows, it can become a searchable library of original notes, curated resources, PDFs, current-affairs analysis, answer-writing guidance and student-focused wellbeing content.
